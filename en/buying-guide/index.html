Buying guide for Phuket property

Ten guides covering ownership rules, process, costs, due diligence and development, written for buyers from outside Thailand.

Information on this website is provided for general guidance only and is not legal, tax or investment advice. Foreign ownership of land in Thailand is restricted by law. Always take independent advice from a licensed Thai lawyer before signing or paying anything.
