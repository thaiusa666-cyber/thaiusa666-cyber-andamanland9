Land for sale in Kata

Kata is a west-coast beach town south of Karon, with a compact centre, a surf beach and hillside roads that lead to viewpoints over the bay.

Demand is driven by tourism, which supports short-stay rental use where regulations allow.

What land here typically looks like

Hillside plots with views and smaller plots in the built-up area behind the beach.

Nearby attractions

  • Kata and Kata Noi beaches
  • Karon to the north
  • Karon viewpoint
  • Chalong and the main road east

Frequently asked questions

Can I rent out a property in Kata short term?

Short-term rental in Thailand is regulated by hotel licensing and other rules. Confirm the position for your specific plan with a licensed Thai lawyer before you buy.

Is parking an issue on hillside plots?

Steep access and turning space are practical constraints worth checking on site.

Are plots here suitable for boutique projects?

Some are. Size, access and zoning determine what can realistically be built.
